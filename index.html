Ingredients

  • 1 cup Basmati rice
  • 500g Chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 Onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/4 cup Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Red chili powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Cooking Instructions
  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry the onions until golden brown. Remove half for garnishing.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for a minute.
  4. Add chicken pieces and cook until they turn white.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt. Cook until tomatoes are soft.
  6. Add yogurt and cook for another 5 minutes.
  7. In a separate pot, boil water and cook the soaked rice until 70% done. Drain the water.
  8. Layer the chicken mixture and partially cooked rice in a pot. Sprinkle garam masala and fried onions on top.
  9. Cover and cook on low heat for 20-25 minutes until the rice is fully cooked.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves before serving.
